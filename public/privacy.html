Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

TL;DR: MindLock stores your data on your device. We don't collect personal information, sell your data, or track you. The only network calls are to generate your personalized daily content.

1. Introduction

MindLock ("we," "our," or "the app") is a mindset and focus app that helps you build better daily habits through personalized content and screen time management. Your privacy matters to us — this policy explains exactly what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

Data stored on your device only (never sent to our servers):

  • Your name (entered during onboarding)
  • Selected goals, blockers, and preferences
  • Mood check-in history
  • Streak and unlock history
  • Bookmarked content
  • Daily micro-actions and intentions
  • Theme and appearance preferences (accent color, background, display mode)
  • App lock settings and schedule

Data sent to our content service (anonymized):

  • Your selected goals and current mood — used solely to generate personalized motivational content
  • Search queries on the Explore page — used to find relevant content from our curated library via semantic matching
  • No identifying information (name, email, device ID) is sent with content requests

Data we do NOT collect:

  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Browsing history
  • Screen time or app usage details (managed by Apple's Screen Time API on-device)
  • Any data for advertising or analytics purposes

3. How We Use Your Data

Your on-device preferences are used exclusively to personalize your experience within the app. When you perform an unlock, your selected goals and current mood are sent to our content generation service to create a personalized motivational message, micro-action, and daily content. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers.

4. AI-Generated Content

MindLock uses AI to generate personalized motivational content during your daily check-in. Your selected goals and current mood are sent to our content service, which uses Google's Gemini API to create a personalized quote, affirmation, or micro-action. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers.

Important: MindLock is a motivational and accountability tool, not a substitute for professional mental health care. It does not provide medical, therapeutic, or clinical advice, and it cannot diagnose any condition. If you are in crisis, please contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988.

5. Screen Time & App Blocking

MindLock may use Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls framework) to manage app restrictions on your device. All Screen Time data is processed entirely on your device by Apple's frameworks. We never have access to which apps you use, how long you use them, or any Screen Time data.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (edge functions) — processes content generation requests. No personal data is stored. Privacy policy
  • Google Gemini API — generates personalized motivational content based on anonymized inputs. Terms of service
  • Apple StoreKit — handles subscriptions and payments. All billing is managed by Apple. Apple Privacy Policy

7. Subscriptions & Payments

MindLock offers subscription plans managed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. All billing, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

8. Data Retention

Since your data is stored on your device, it exists only as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling MindLock removes all stored data. We do not retain any data on our servers — content generation requests are processed in real-time and immediately discarded.

9. Children's Privacy

MindLock is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will take steps to address the issue.

10. Your Rights

Because your data lives on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app
  • Delete: Uninstall the app to remove all data
  • Portability: Your data stays on your device and is not locked to any account

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
